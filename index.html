Ways to contribute

The initiative is volunteer-driven. Every catalog, group, and talk on this site came from someone taking one of these steps.

  • Add your workflow systemGet it profiled in the systems catalog, refreshed automatically from your repository
  • Join a working groupShape benchmarking, FAIR workflows, quantum-classical, or terminology
  • Give a community talkPresent your system or research in the monthly virtual series
  • Contribute a registryHelp others discover pipelines that already exist
  • List a research frameworkShare a project building on workflow technology
  • Become a memberJoin 323 people across 27 countries
